Best texts

Which outbound texts get the best replies, across all your sub-accounts

A reply is the contact's first text back within 3 days, before the next text you sent them. Names, amounts, links and numbers are swapped for {placeholders} so the same message sent to many people counts as one. Ranking uses a confidence-adjusted rate, so a text sent 30 times with 9 positive replies ranks above one sent 3 times with 2. Deals won = the contact had a deal marked won within 90 days of the text.
