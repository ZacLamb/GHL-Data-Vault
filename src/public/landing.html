What you get

Every file, every source

Contact and opportunity file fields, conversation attachments, call recordings and voicemails, form and survey uploads, the media library, signed documents.

A manifest you can join

Each file is tied to its contact, opportunity, conversation or submission — so the export matches the CSVs you already have.

Monthly, across the agency

Pick a day and hour. Every sub-account gets an incremental backup; new sub-accounts join automatically. Jobs resume after restarts and respect GHL's daily API quota.

Rep packages and pushes

Split a sub-account's contacts into non-overlapping packages with their files, or push contacts + documents into another sub-account through the API.

Master search

Search every backed-up sub-account at once by name, email, phone, filename or any custom field value. Find the same person across sub-accounts.

Usage analytics

Per-sub-account and per-user activity: messages, calls, talk time, deals, idle seats. Printable owner reports with a share link.
